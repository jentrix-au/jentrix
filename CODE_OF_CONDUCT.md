# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit and the standards of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. The short version:

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not with people. No harassment,
  discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing of others' private
  information, anywhere this project's work happens — issues, pull requests,
  discussions, and any channel that represents the project.
- **Be useful.** Assume good faith, give and accept constructive review, and
  keep the conversation about the change in front of you.
- **Take responsibility.** If you got it wrong, say so and fix it.

Maintainers may edit, remove or decline any contribution, comment or issue
that does not meet these standards, and may temporarily or permanently ban a
participant for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or
harmful. Enforcement follows the Covenant's ladder: correction, warning,
temporary ban, permanent ban.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately — the code owners
named in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](./.github/CODEOWNERS) are reachable through
their GitHub profiles, and a report about a maintainer may be sent to any
other maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially and reporters are not
retaliated against.
